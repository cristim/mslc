Qualified missing Metal members currently lose their namespace identity and can call an unrelated global helper. For example, `metal::ghost(input)` or `N::ghost(input)` through a Metal using directive can reach global `ghost` even though Apple rejects the qualified member.

This change preserves canonical qualified member identity until intrinsic dispatch. Exact owned Metal helpers, struct/enum/type owners and ordinary unqualified or explicitly global helpers retain their own identity. Intrinsics recognize supported canonical names without flattening unknown members into global helper, constant or type lookup.
